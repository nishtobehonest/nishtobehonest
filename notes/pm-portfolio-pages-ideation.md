# PM Portfolio — Pages to Ideate On (Future)

Status: parking lot / ideation. Not a commitment. Source: a "what pages should a modern PM portfolio include" brainstorm.
Captured: 2026-08-31.

Context: `nishtobehonest` is already the living portfolio (knowledge-graph site, positioning
= Agentic PM · FDE). This doc is a menu of *content sections* worth pulling in over time —
whether as new nodes in `data/nodes.json`, new panels, or a companion Notion space for the
long-form stuff that doesn't belong on the canvas.

---

## Candidate sections

### 1. Product Thinking
- Case studies: past product decisions, tradeoffs, user impact
- Problem statements solved
- Competitive teardown examples
- Product redesign walkthroughs
- Frameworks customized or developed
- **Signals:** product sense, interview-ready depth

### 2. AI-Augmented Workflows
- How I've used LLMs / automation in real work
- Before/after workflows (ticket triage, PRD writing, roadmap planning)
- Reusable prompt libraries
- AI + PM tool stack
- **Signals:** AI-native, forward-looking (this is core to my positioning)

### 3. Metrics & Impact
- Product adoption visuals
- Dashboards built or tracked
- How success metrics were defined and iterated (pre/post-launch)
- **Signals:** outcome driver, not just a planner

### 4. Roadmaps & Strategy
- Annotated roadmap screenshots with rationale
- Prioritization frameworks used (RICE, MoSCoW, etc.)
- Stakeholder alignment stories
- **Signals:** strategic thinking, comfort with ambiguity

### 5. Learning Vault
- Notes from key books / newsletters / podcasts
- Project reflections (what I'd do differently)
- Templates: idea validation, product discovery, retros
- **Signals:** growth mindset, learning machine

### 6. People & Process
- How I ran rituals: standups, sprint reviews, OKRs
- Conflict resolution scenarios
- Team onboarding docs / playbooks
- **Signals:** leadership, EQ, process excellence

### 7. Personal Branding / About Me
- Career narrative and values
- Timeline of key moments (jobs, side projects, pivots)
- LinkedIn + resume + contact
- **Signals:** the human, for hiring managers and networking
- *(Portfolio already has an ABOUT panel — extend, don't duplicate)*

### 8. Passion Projects & Side Hustles
- Poker, e-commerce, any tools/products shipped
- Blog posts, videos, templates
- Screenshots, testimonials, learnings
- **Signals:** makes me stand out, feels real

### 9. Product Interview Prep Hub *(optional, only while job hunting)*
- Company-specific research pages
- Mock interview recordings
- STAR examples and PM stories
- Estimation / case practice notes
- *(Keep private — likely lives in `career/interview-prep/`, not the public site)*

## Reference — the shift in what PM hiring looks for

> Product Manager hiring is evolving — at least for me.
>
> From now on, I want to see what's in a PM's toolbox:
> - What's in your prompt library?
> - What automations have you built to increase your speed and leverage?
> - What's in your GitHub?
> - What prototypes or passion projects show how you think and build?
> - What AI tools have you mastered and why?
>
> The jobs will still be won with product taste, listening and empathy, the ability to
> create clarity and insight, and good old-fashioned work ethic. But if you don't have your
> own tools and practical knowledge, you're probably not ready. These are now the ante.

**Why this matters for the portfolio:** every bullet above is a section this doc already
lists (prompt library → #2, automations → #2, GitHub → #7/#8, prototypes/passion projects →
#8, AI tools mastered → #2). The takeaway: these aren't "nice to have" pages — they're table
stakes, and the site should make them impossible to miss.
